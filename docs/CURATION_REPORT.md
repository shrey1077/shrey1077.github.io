# CURATION REPORT — Tata IIS

Curated like a museum exhibition, not a file browser. Ratings answer one
question: **how much of the visitor's attention does this deserve?**

Scale: ★★★★★ Hero Experience · ★★★★ Main Portfolio · ★★★ Supporting
Collection · ★★ Archive · ★ Internal Reference.

## ★★★★★ Hero Experiences

| Collection | Why |
|---|---|
| **Logo Guidelines** | The intellectual core of the whole engagement. The construction pages expose real design mathematics (8x grid, 2x separation, 24x width, the divider rule) — proof of *system thinking*, not decoration. With the actual intro renders (MP4) and the master PDF, this becomes the Interactive Logo Construction experience — the single strongest "explain the thinking" artifact in the archive. |
| **Tata Quotes (Legacy boards)** | Jamsetji, JRD, and Ratan Tata — designed quote boards with portraits, already produced for campus walls. This is the emotional foundation: the Legacy sequence opens the memory with the values the institute inherits. Museum material in the most literal sense. |
| **Brochures** | The flagship publications. A visible evolution ("First brochures" → FINAL 2024 → August 2025, funding editions, web versions) tells a story of a brand maturing — process AND outcome, exactly what the creative intent demands. |
| **Videos** | Motion is where the identity comes alive (logo renders: Cogs 3D, Tech Simple; campus films: Additive Manufacturing timelapse, Skills Conclave). A curated video wall gives the experience a heartbeat no still can. |

## The curator's cuts (principles applied)

1. **One strong example beats six variants.** Certificates: show the system
   via 3–4 artifacts, not 45 files. Mockups: ~8 of 41. Flyers: 6–8 of 57.
2. **Process earns wall space.** The brochure evolution and the logo
   construction pages are shown *as process* — before/after, grid overlays.
3. **Privacy is non-negotiable.** Anything with a personal name or business
   detail (CV, New Joinee, placement proposal, student certificates with real
   names if any) never renders.
4. **The gap is admitted, not disguised.** No photography exists; the
   experience never fakes it (no stock). Photography & Media leads with film.

Target: **~60–80 curated artifacts** on display out of ~440 files (≈15–18%) —
the right ratio for an exhibition.
