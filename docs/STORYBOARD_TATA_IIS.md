# STORYBOARD — The Tata IIS Memory

Directed like a short film in nine scenes. No code here — only what the
visitor sees, feels, and hears (silence; this film has no soundtrack, only
pacing). Every scene obeys the constitution: calm, precise, museum-quality;
the transition IS the story; never a loading spinner.

---

## SCENE 0 — The Mind at Rest
*The homepage. The brain turns almost imperceptibly with the visitor's hand.*
Eight thoughts wait around the two hemispheres, handwritten → set type.
Nothing asks to be clicked. The visitor chooses **Clients → Tata IIS**.

## SCENE 1 — Retrieval *(the Memory Transition)*
The page does not "navigate". The other thoughts quieten (fade a step back).
From the chosen card, a hairline draws back toward the LEFT hemisphere — the
logic side, where client work lives — and the brain leans toward it, the way
it already leans toward hovered thoughts. A white veil rises like recall
washing over vision. **Cut to white.** Duration: under two seconds, ease-out,
reversible in memory. *(Architecture: ExperienceTransition + MemoryOverlay +
BrainConnection — already scaffolded.)*

## SCENE 2 — Legacy *(plays once, ever)*
White. Then, one at a time, three figures surface — sepia portraits on white,
each with a single line and nothing else:

1. **Jamsetji Tata** — *"Always aim at perfection in whatever you do.
   There's always something that could be done better."*
2. **J. R. D. Tata** — his line, from the JRD quote board.
3. **Ratan Tata** — his line, from the RTN board.

Each holds ~3 seconds: portrait settles, the quote writes itself
(thought-voice → set type), a two-digit year-range whispers underneath.
No biography. Values, inherited. A quiet **Skip** sits bottom-right for
returning visitors' respect; a local flag ensures the sequence never plays
twice. *(Source: the archive's own Tata Quotes boards.)*

## SCENE 3 — Arrival: the Name
The veil thins. On white, a faint construction grid fades in — and the
**TATA IIS** wordmark assembles on it: baseline rule first, then the serif
letters, then the divider bar, then TATA INDIAN INSTITUTE OF SKILLS beneath.
Today this is a composed reveal; the full guidelines-driven construction
animation (8x/24x math, measured callouts) arrives in the next milestone —
the stage is built for it. The eyebrow reads: *Client — Memory 01.*

## SCENE 4 — Design DNA
Four principles surface in sequence, each a word pair and one quiet sentence:
**Industry 4.0 · Precision · Human Potential · Scalable Brand System.**
Behind "Precision", the zebra motif from the guidelines ghosts past; behind
"Scalable", the orange rings. Editable content, not hardcoded copy.

## SCENE 5 — Campus Network
The parent mark sits center. Two hairlines grow downward-left and
downward-right — **Ahmedabad** and **Mumbai** — each terminating in its
navy/orange dot-tree campus logo. The lines draw themselves (the nav's
rule-and-dot language at architectural scale). One line of context per campus.

## SCENE 6 — The Communication Ecosystem *(the heart)*
The six systems present themselves as an exhibition floor plan — six quiet
cards, each asking its question: *Who are we? How do we speak? What does it
look like alive? How do we live on screens? What does a student hold? What
else does the mind make?* Hovering a system breathes it open a few pixels;
choosing one expands its curated collections in place (the homepage sheet's
language, inherited).

## SCENE 7 — Collections *(each in its own voice)*
- **Logo System** — the construction pages step through like plates in a
  monograph; the real intro render plays once, small and confident.
- **Publications** — brochure spreads page like a book on a table.
- **Films** — a dark-less video wall: posters, one player at a time.
- **The Credential** — a certificate that turns over in the hand (front/back),
  its variants fanned behind it.
- **Brand Literacy** — story frames advance like Instagram held at arm's
  length.
- **The Brand in the World** — full-bleed mockups, one per viewport, slow.
- Grids (posters, flyers, stationery still-life) stay grids — quiet, even,
  three columns, generous margins.

## SCENE 8 — Artifact
Any artifact opens in the white viewer: the piece large, its name in mono
meta, nothing else. Escape returns. (MediaViewer — built.)

## SCENE 9 — Surfacing
At the memory's end: *Previous memory / Next memory* — or **Back**, which
raises the veil for half a beat and returns to the mind. The brain is exactly
where the visitor left it. The thread that pulled them in is gone; the memory
is now theirs too.

---

### Pacing chart (feel, not milliseconds)
Retrieval **fast-then-soft** · Legacy **slow, held** · Arrival **confident** ·
DNA **rhythmic** · Network **drawn** · Ecosystem **calm** · Collections
**unhurried** · Surfacing **gentle**.

### What this storyboard refuses
Loading spinners. Autoplaying sound. Parallax theatrics. Stock photography.
Biography recitals. Showing all 440 files.
